Our Story

A Decade of Search Excellence

SEOCOM was born from a simple observation: too many SEO agencies promise results but deliver vanity metrics. We wanted to build something different — a firm obsessed with revenue impact, not just rankings.

Our founders spent years inside Google's search infrastructure before launching SEOCOM in 2014. That insider perspective, combined with a relentless focus on ROI, has made us one of the most trusted SEO consultancies in North America.

Today, we partner with over 200 businesses — from ambitious startups to Fortune 500 enterprises — delivering SEO strategies that move the bottom line.
